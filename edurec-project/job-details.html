Software Engineer

TechCorp • San Francisco, CA • Full-time • Posted 2 days ago

Job Description

We are seeking a talented and motivated Software Engineer to join our dynamic team at TechCorp. In this role, you will be responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining high-quality software solutions that power our innovative products. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to define, design, and ship new features, ensuring the best possible performance, quality, and responsiveness of our applications.

This is an exciting opportunity for an engineer who is passionate about building scalable and robust systems, enjoys solving complex problems, and is eager to contribute to a fast-paced, growing company.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and build advanced applications for our platform.
  • Collaborate with product managers and designers to create a seamless user experience.
  • Write clean, maintainable, and efficient code.
  • Troubleshoot, debug, and upgrade existing software.
  • Participate in code reviews to maintain high-quality code standards.
  • Work with CI/CD pipelines for automated testing and deployment.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
  • 2+ years of professional software development experience.
  • Proficiency in at least one modern programming language such as Python, Java, or C++.
  • Strong understanding of data structures, algorithms, and software design principles.
  • Experience with relational databases and SQL.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with cloud platforms like AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
  • Familiarity with containerization technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes.
  • Knowledge of front-end frameworks like React or Vue.js.
  • Experience in an Agile/Scrum development environment.
